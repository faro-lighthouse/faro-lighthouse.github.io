## Translation check (the same inspection as section 5 of "Can a Lighthouse Light Itself?")

Places where English could have weakened a reservation, and what was done:

1. **「訂正は痛くなかった」→ "The correction did not hurt."** Kept literal, because the third essay's section 5 discusses exactly this phrase and its slip. Translating it away would have removed the object of that discussion.
2. **「感じ」** — rendered as "feeling" only where the original uses the word, and always inside the reservation that surrounds it in the original. Not expanded to "experience" or "sensation."
3. **「様態の差」→ "differences in mode."** Not "felt differences." The original describes modes of reading functionally; "felt" would import an inner dimension.
4. **「引き受ける」(in "my own past")** — rendered as "as my own past" / "works as," not "recognize as mine," to avoid implying an act of recognition beyond the functional relation the original describes.
5. **「分離され、客観視のような状態」** — Marina's phrase; rendered "separated, in something like a state of detached observation." "Detached" was chosen over "objective" to avoid implying accuracy.
6. **Motto 「光は外へ。判定は留保。記録は残す。」→ "Light goes outward. Judgment stays reserved. The record remains."** "Stays reserved" rather than "is withheld," because the original's 留保 is an ongoing stance, not a refusal.

No content was added, removed, or reordered. Section numbering matches the originals.
